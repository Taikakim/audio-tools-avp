# SA3 Control — Research Radar

Stable Audio 3 is a rectified-flow latent DiT: SAME-L autoencoder → 256-d latent stream (T=4096, 10.767 Hz) → DiT with T5-Gemma cross-attention, single global sigma walking noise→clean, CFG as the main dial. This radar collects 46 verified, adaptable research components — methods and mechanisms, mostly from image/video/LLM diffusion — that hook into one of SA3's six injection points: **(a)** prompt/conditioner & cross-attention, **(b)** guidance/sampling, **(c)** latent during sampling, **(d)** weights, **(e)** autoencoder/latent geometry, **(f)** closed-loop/search. We already have LatCH heads, FusionOpt, FlowEdit+RF-Solver, a FIFO long-form prototype, and LoRA finetuning; everything below is **adjacent or better**. Entries are grouped by hook and ordered by promise × ease; each carries an effort rating (low/med/high) and the primary source. Recurring SA3 constraints: guidance must run **fp32** (fp16 clashes with backprop grad dtypes); 1D temporal latents (not 2D spatial) so all "spatial mask" tricks become **temporal windowing**; SA3 uses **descending-t** Euler.

## 1. Guidance & Sampling (hook b) — swap the CFG/velocity update rule

These are mostly drop-in replacements for the CFG combination line in `sampling.py`, zero or near-zero extra training. The first three are the cheapest high-value wins.

- **CFG-Zero\*** — per-step optimal scalar `s* = (v_cond·v_uncond)/‖v_uncond‖²` on the CFG velocity, plus zero-init of the first ODE step. — Fixes early-step (high-t, t≈1) CFG misdirection that drives tonal-centre drift; the zero-init is a one-line change and is exactly where SA3's FIFO frame-boundary noise misbehaves. **Validated on SD3/Flux/Lumina (same RF-DiT family).** — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18886
- **APG (Adaptive Projected Guidance)** — decompose CFG diff into parallel/orthogonal components w.r.t. the conditional, down-weight the parallel (oversaturation) term via η, add negative momentum. — Lets SA3 run higher CFG without saturation collapse, zero extra NFE. **Already partly in-repo** (`dit.py` `apg_project()` + `apg_scale`); only the **negative-momentum** term is missing — finishing it is the cheapest open item here. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02416
- **ERG (Entropy Rectifying Guidance)** — temperature τ on the self-attention softmax (Hopfield-energy view) gives a "weak prediction" with no separate model; 2 NFE/step like CFG. — DiT-native, sampler-agnostic, single continuous dial easy to schedule across timesteps; composes with APG. Validated on DiT-XL and MMDiT. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13987
- **TPG (Token Perturbation Guidance)** — guidance contrast from a second pass with norm-preserving token shuffle (permutation), no uncond model needed; works even fully unconditional. — Its spectrum matches CFG's (steers content, not just sharpness); unconditional mode = guidance for **LatCH-only generation with no text prompt**. Tested on SD3; shuffle is index-agnostic so 1D audio tokens are fine. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10036
- **Autoguidance (bad version of itself)** — replace the CFG uncond branch with a forward pass through a *weaker* checkpoint of the same model. — SA3 already ships candidates: `small-music` or `medium-base` (vs finetuned `medium`), or an early LoRA step — better gradient direction than the null-prompt "average audio," preserves diversity. Cost: two checkpoints in VRAM. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02507
- **Rectified-CFG++** — predictor-corrector guidance derived specifically for RF; couples a conditional RF step with a geometry-aware correction to stay on-manifold and keep the ODE invertible. — Theoretically grounded fix for SA3's high-CFG mode-collapse; **also restores inversion fidelity** → directly helps RF-Solver separation. Must re-derive the corrector (can't cargo-cult from DDPM). NeurIPS 2025, no audio yet. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07631
- **TFG (Unified Training-Free Guidance)** — meta-framework unifying DPS/MPGD/FreeDoM/LGD/UGD into 4 axes (mean μ, variance ρ, smoothing γ̄, recurrence) + auto hyperparameter search. — Replaces our brittle hand-tuned LatCH gain (48–96) with a principled design space + search. **`latch_guided.py` already implements the RF adaptation** (variance grad on x_t, mean grad on z0=x−t·v); this systematizes the tuning. Public code. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15761

## 2. Latent-Nudge & Test-Time Optimization (hooks c, f) — gradient/search on the latent toward an audio target

Plug a (differentiable or black-box) audio feature/network as the loss; steer the latent during sampling or optimize x_T directly. This is the closest cluster to LatCH and the richest near-term experiment space.

- **FreeDoM** — backprop the gradient of any *pretrained* time-independent network through x0-hat to the noisy latent; no head training. — **Skips LatCH's head-training step**: plug a MIR network (onset, RMS, spectral centroid), energy = MSE(predicted feature, target), backprop. RF produces an x0-hat too, so the chain is identical. fp32 + `enable_grad` required. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09833
- **MPGD (Manifold-Preserving Guided Diffusion)** — wrap any classifier/energy with an AE-shortcut manifold projection: decode z0-hat→audio via SAME-L, compute loss, gradient through the decoder (MPGD-LDM variant). — **Uses SA3's own SAME-L decoder** so off-the-shelf audio classifiers (CLAP, beat tracker, Audiobox Aesthetics) become guidance losses without pushing the latent off-manifold; 3.8× speedup vs naive. Solves the "raw latent gradient → garbage audio" problem. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16424
- **FlowDPS** — DPS-style posterior sampling adapted to RF via flow-Tweedie; clean-signal branch gets the measurement gradient, noise branch perturbs; per Euler step, no retraining. — Built *for* latent RF-DiTs (SD3-class). "Measurement" = any differentiable audio feature (LUFS, BPM energy, chroma-to-reference). More principled than LatCH TFG because it respects flow geometry. Backprop through SAME-L decoder → fp32. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08136
- **DITTO / DITTO-2** — optimize initial noise x_T against a differentiable feature loss (melody/intensity/structure/CLAP) through the full denoise chain; DITTO-2 distills a 1-step consistency surrogate for 10–20× speedup. — **The most complete audio test-time-latent-opt recipe**; controls map straight to our MIR features. Base DITTO works immediately with gradient checkpointing; DITTO-2 needs an SA3 consistency surrogate (RF consistency distillation, non-trivial). RF's straight trajectories are friendlier to backprop than DDPM. — effort **med** (DITTO) / **high** (DITTO-2 distill) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12179 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20289
- **DAS (Diffusion Alignment as Sampling, SMC)** — wrap sampling in a Sequential Monte Carlo loop: N parallel trajectories, per-step reweight by a tempered reward, resample; prevents reward over-optimization. — **Black-box rewards** (onset strength, beat-grid corr, LUFS, Audiobox) with no gradient; composes with existing LoRA. Caveat: needs SDE-ification of SA3's Euler ODE (small noise injection) to form a valid SMC target; N=8–16 feasible on 16 GB if run sequentially per step. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05803
- **TreeG (tree search over denoise steps)** — propose N candidate continuations per step, score each (black-box reward on x0-hat), keep K best, branch; inference-time scaling law. — **Validated on symbolic music (+29%)**, non-differentiable rewards OK, RF-compatible. Maps onto SA3's Euler loop: branch at intermediate sigma, score LatCH head on x0-hat, prune. Cost = branching × DiT passes (prototype on `small-music`/CPU). — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11420
- **DOODL** — full-chain backprop via an invertible (EDICT) process at O(1) memory; any frozen classifier as loss on x_T. — Audio analogue of DITTO with exact gradients. Caveat: EDICT's constant-memory property is **DDIM-specific and does not port to RF** out of the box; substitute an RF-reversible integrator (the in-repo RF-Solver run in reverse) or just use gradient checkpointing. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13703

## 3. Adapters & Weights (hooks a, d) — finetune/LoRA/RL beyond plain LoRA

From cheap drop-in LoRA variants through contrastive sliders to RL finetuning. Several reuse SA3's existing `load_lora`/`set_lora_strength` plumbing verbatim.

- **Concept Sliders** — train a tiny LoRA on contrastive prompt pairs (A→B) with preservation prompts to isolate a *single continuous axis*; sliders compose linearly. — **Drop-in to SA3's LoRA API**: one slider per MIR feature (bass prominence, BPM feel, reverb density), stack independently, modulate at runtime with `set_lora_strength`. We already have prompt pairs + RMS/BPM labels. The compositional/preservation framing is the underused insight vs holistic "style LoRA." — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12092
- **VeRA** — one frozen random low-rank matrix pair shared across all layers; train only tiny per-layer scaling vectors b, d. 10–100× fewer params than LoRA. — Cuts SA3 finetune cost (T=4096, fp32-forced); each slider = two tiny vectors/layer, composition = vector addition. Occupies the same weight slots as LoRA → loader needs only to store/reproduce the RNG seed. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11454
- **FreeSliders** — training-free concept sliders: `v_steered = v_base + μ·(v_pos − v_neg)` from 3 forward passes/step (base, +concept, −concept). — **Benchmarked on Stable Audio Open, 10 attributes, +17% vs trained sliders.** Zero training; adds one pass over our existing CFG. Continuous dial for hard-to-verbalize attributes; composes with LoRA+FIFO. (Hook spans b/c — it's a multi-guidance operator.) — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00103
- **MuseControlLite** — decoupled cross-attention adapters **with RoPE** on a frozen music DiT for time-varying control (melody/rhythm/dynamics); 85M params (~8%). — **Built directly on Stable Audio Open** (same SAME AE + DiT family). The exact recipe SA3 LatCH-phase-2 needs to consume per-frame targets; our `latents_sa3/*.TIMESERIES.npz` (beat/onset/RMS/chroma per frame) is ready-made training data. RoPE shown *essential* for temporal alignment. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18729
- **GLIGEN gated self-attention** — insert new gated (tanh γ, init 0 = identity) self-attention layers per block to ingest structured non-text tokens; freeze original cross-attn. — Inject a **second modality** (beat-grid, reference-audio SAME latents, MIR timeseries) per temporal frame without overwriting T5-Gemma prompt-following; gate doubles as a CFG-like dial. SA3's `TransformerBlock` exposes clean self/cross-attn hooks. Needs an MLP projector + training. — effort **high** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.07093
- **DDPO (RL as MDP, REINFORCE/PPO)** — treat each denoise step as an action, score final audio with any reward, policy-gradient finetune (LoRA-able). — **Non-differentiable rewards** (run a beat tracker, count misaligned onsets). Caveat: original is DDPM-only (needs per-step Gaussian densities); for SA3's RF, add SDE noise to rollouts (standard Flow-GRPO/DanceGRPO pattern) or do reward-weighted SFT on the final output. — effort **high** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13301
- **Flow-GRPO** — GRPO (the DeepSeek-R1 algorithm) for flow-matching: sample K outputs/prompt, group-normalize advantages, update LoRA; no critic. GRPO-Guard prevents ratio blow-up. — **Cheaper than DDPO** (no value net); reward = any mir-venv tool. Official code uses LoRA r=32 on SD3.5-M/FLUX (same RF family). Caveat: ODE→SDE noise injection + audio reward functions from scratch; K-sampling expensive on 16 GB → use `small-music-base`/short crops. — effort **high** — https://github.com/yifan123/flow_grpo

## 4. Per-Frame Sigma & Long-Form (hooks b, c) — break the single global timestep

Directly upgrades our FIFO/diagonal-denoising prototype (`fifo_infinite.py`, per-frame timestep monkey-patch already present). Inference-only tricks first; training objectives and architectural anchors after.

- **FIFO-Diffusion (latent partitioning + lookahead denoising)** — split the queue into sub-blocks with narrower per-block sigma ranges; update only the noisiest half so every frame denoises against clean-enough neighbours. — **Closes the train/inference gap** of diagonal denoising with zero finetuning (~50 LOC). Latent partitioning already ≈ `build_slot_sigmas()`; **lookahead is NOT yet implemented** in our prototype — straightforward to add (skip Euler update below a sigma cutoff). Could turn the untested FIFO into a working system in one experiment. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11473
- **Rolling Diffusion** — position-relative sigma ramp `t_w = (w+t)/W` (later positions = higher sigma); window slides forward, one clean frame/step. — Clean, motivated per-frame sigma assignment with the right FIFO shape (head≈clean, tail=noise); validated on chaotic fluid dynamics (modality-agnostic). Drop-in swap for our linear sigma ramp; measure crossfade coherence. Inference-only use is heuristic (model wasn't trained with it). — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09470
- **Deep Forcing (Deep Sink + Participative Compression)** — training-free streaming KV-cache mgmt: persistent anchor frames with **temporal RoPE phase re-alignment** to the current position; prune cache by recent attention weight. — Anchors style/key/energy without motion stagnation (the RoPE re-align is the fix); Participative Compression keeps musically salient frames (downbeats, key changes), drops sustained-note redundancy. SA3 is RoPE-encoded → ports directly, training-free, testable on `medium` now. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05081
- **Diffusion Forcing** — train with independent per-token noise levels (VLB over all subsequences) → causal/AR generation + arbitrary subsequence conditioning. — **The principled training objective our FIFO prototype approximates at inference.** Closes the train/inference gap; per-position sigma → per-position flow-time t_i in RF. Needs RF re-derivation of the VLB + a finetune from `medium-base` (20k steps shown viable elsewhere). — effort **high** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01392
- **Rolling Forcing** — rolling-window sigma + bidirectional in-window attention + dual-tier KV cache (recent context + initial anchor frames as attention sink) with dynamic RoPE re-alignment. — **Lowest drift at real-time** (ΔDrift≈0.01 vs 1.66 Self-Forcing); attention-sink + dynamic RoPE is the missing piece for long-form SA3 anchoring. Rolling sigma + in-window attn are sampler-only; the dual KV anchor needs a short long-audio finetune + a causal mask (SA3 has none by default). — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25161

## 5. Interpretability → Steering Directions (hooks c, e) — read internal structure, turn it into a knob

Activation steering and sparse autoencoders give *additive offset* control — cheaper than gradient guidance and complementary to LatCH (different signal, different failure modes). The TADA cluster is the single most directly-applicable group here (validated on Stable Audio Open).

- **TADA (activation steering on audio-DiT bottleneck)** — activation patching finds a small set of consecutive attention layers (a "semantic bottleneck"); inject Contrastive Activation Addition (mean-difference) vectors there at inference, no retraining. — **Validated on Stable Audio Open** (SA3's sibling): bottleneck at layers {11,12}/24, controls vocals/tempo/genre/instruments. ~256 contrast pairs, one-liner injection, outperforms LoRA/prompt-tuning on attribute precision. **Strongest empirical prior in this whole radar.** (Note: "ReNorm" in some descriptions is not in the paper — it uses CAA + SAEs.) — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11910
- **SAE on SAME-L latents (pitch/timbre/loudness)** — train a sparse autoencoder on the autoencoder latent corpus to split polysemantic dims into monosemantic acoustic features → linear knobs. — **We have the dataset**: 5400 crops `(1,256,4096)` fp16 on NVMe; SAE run = hours. Validated on DiffRhythm-VAE/EnCodec (pitch/amplitude/timbre linearly separable). Pure latent geometry, no diffusion involvement. Caveats: SAME-L compat is extrapolation; onset features unproven (paper shows pitch/amp/timbre only). — effort **low** — https://openreview.net/forum?id=5fsYFQzzMX (arXiv 2510.23802)
- **DiffMean steering on music transformers** — difference-in-means steering vectors from hidden states; one-to-all injection (best-layer vector added across all layers) with scalar λ. — Discover linear directions for BPM/key/density/energy; complementary to LatCH (mean-diff of activations vs gradient-of-head). Demonstrated on MusicGen (AR); for SA3 replace EOS-token anchor with conditioning-token or latent pooling, inject across denoise steps. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04479
- **SHIFT (steer hidden intermediates in flow DiTs)** — dual injection: a vector into the text-encoder pooled embedding (hook a) + one into DiT text-token activations (hook c), with cosine-sim **dynamic per-step scaling** (amplify if concept absent, suppress if present). — The dynamic scaling directly addresses SA3's over-guidance at high noise. Hook (a) (T5-Gemma embedding) maps cleanly; hook (c) re-targets SA3's cross-attn context (paper is joint-attn MMDiT, needs re-derivation). Mean-difference vectors, cheap. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09213
- **SMITIN (self-monitored intervention)** — logistic-regression probes on individual attention heads; shift activations along the probe direction, with a **self-monitoring loop that backs off when it detects incoherence**. — The self-monitor directly solves **LatCH-at-high-gain oversteer**. We have the labels (4800 crops: BPM/key/RMS/downbeats). MusicGen-only so far; re-ground the monitor on probe confidence across T latent positions per step. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02252
- **TIDE (temporal-aware SAE on DiT activations)** — timestep-conditioned SAE (scale/shift from the model's own AdaLN) finds monosemantic features evolving across denoise steps (structure early → semantics mid → detail late); erase/scale/transfer them. — **SA3 uses AdaLN** → the temporal-modulation trick fits natively. Hypothesis: early t = timbral/structural, late t = melodic/harmonic detail. Post-hoc on frozen activations, no retraining. Caveat: no audio experiments exist; penultimate block is the sweet spot. — effort **high** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07050

## 6. Attention Editing & Inversion (hooks a, b, c) — structure-preserving edits, prompt fidelity, faithful inversion

Self/cross-attention injection and RF inversion solvers — the toolkit for SA3 editing, separation, inpainting, and tighter round-trips. Several directly upgrade the existing FlowEdit/RF-Solver separation pipeline.

- **FireFlow** — RF ODE solver with second-order accuracy at Euler cost by reusing the velocity field across midpoint steps; 8-step inversion, 3× faster. — Drop-in for the inversion pass in `sa3_zerosep_rf.py`; tightens the current 0.23 round-trip rel-error and frees budget for more steps → better FlowEdit/AUDEDIT + LatCH baselines. A few dozen LOC, public repo. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07517
- **Attend-and-Excite** — per-step latent gradient nudge maximizing the *minimum* peak cross-attention over all subject tokens (Gaussian-smoothed maps). — Fixes SA3 **prompt-token neglect** ("kick, bass, hi-hat" → one element missing) — arguably worse in audio where dropped elements are hard to hear/measure. Cheap (one backward over attention weights/step); 1D T=4096 cross-attn map is a direct analogue. Tune iteration count for cost. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13826
- **MasaCtrl (mutual self-attention)** — replace target-branch self-attention K/V with the source branch's during denoise; foreground/background mask derived from cross-attention (no manual mask). — The **non-rigid edit SA3 lacks**: preserve source timbre/texture, reshape content via new prompt (middle ground between P2P and SDEdit). Apply to a suffix of TransformerBlocks (no encoder/decoder split in SA3); mask becomes a text-token→time-segment map. Needs two parallel branches (2× cost). — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08465
- **KV-Edit (background KV injection)** — cache unedited-region KV from one pass, concatenate with foreground queries throughout denoise; O(1) memory, inversion-free variant. — **Hard-anchors** the unchanged context in inpainting/continuation (frozen front/back bars don't drift) — stronger than our sliding-window inpaint that only hopes the boundary holds. 2D spatial mask → 1D temporal mask; choose the audio self-attn sub-block. Demonstrated on MM-DiT. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17363
- **RF-Inversion via LQR (stochastic SDE)** — recast RF inversion as optimal control → reverse rectified-SDE; add `√[2(1-t)(1-η)/t]·dW_t` per step, η dials fidelity↔editability. — **Fixes the MASTER.md sign/blow-up at t→1**: the stochastic term absorbs per-step error and avoids the z0-anchor singularity; replaces our ad-hoc η dial with a principled one. Drop-in for the reverse step in `sa3_zerosep_rf.py`; implemented on Flux (same descending-t convention). — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10792
- **RF-Solver + RF-Edit (attention feature sharing)** — high-order Taylor RF inversion + inject self-attention K/V from the inversion pass into the editing pass to preserve structure. — Gives SA3's FlowEdit a **P2P-style structure memory** it currently lacks — key for separation/stem-editing where non-target sources must stay fixed. Solver ports as a drop-in over the in-repo reverse-Euler; injection needs forward hooks on DiT self-attn (already exposed). Open Q: do audio self-attn features carry structural meaning? — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04746
- **FluxSpace** — inference-time semantic edit directions from joint-attention outputs via orthogonal projection against the null condition; attention-mask-localized interpolation; separate fine/coarse controls. — Orthogonal projection yields a clean LatCH-like direction **without a trained head**; coarse/fine split mirrors SA3's cross-attn. Caveat: FLUX is joint-attn (single stream); SA3 has separate cross-attn → re-engineer the extraction onto cross-attn outputs; spatial mask → temporal window. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09611
- **Stable Flow (vital-layer identification)** — residual-bypass ablation + perceptual distance ranks which DiT layers are "vital"; inject self-attention features only there for structure-preserving edits. — Answers "**which layers?**" for SA3 (no UNet hierarchy to guess from) — a one-time offline ablation with an audio perceptual proxy (CLAP / SAME cosine / mel-MSE) replacing DINOv2. The latent-nudging inverse-Euler component aligns with existing `sa3_zerosep_rf.py`. Re-derive vital indices for SA3. — effort **med** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14430
- **JASCO (information bottleneck + temporal pooling)** — pass time-varying conditions through an IB layer + temporal average-pool (not Gaussian blur) before injection → a leakage dial preventing copy-not-guide. — A principled **over-conditioning regularizer** for noisy MIR targets (chords, onset envelopes, drum refs): bottleneck strength dials "follows tightly ↔ loosely inspired." Architecture-agnostic preprocessing. Caveat: JASCO injects by concatenation (cross-attn was *worse* in its ablation); recalibrate pooling window for SA3's coarse 10.767 Hz grid. — effort **low** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10970
