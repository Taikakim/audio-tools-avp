*Companion to **[Hands Inside the Instrument](README.md)**. ## How to read this

**The two hard filters** decide whether a method transfers at all. Most of the diffusion-control literature fails one of them:

1. **Rectified flow, not DDPM.** SA3's `-base` checkpoints carve along a straight-line velocity field `z_t = (1-t)·z0 + t·noise`. Methods derived for DDPM/score-SDE ("add noise to σ", ancestral sampling, score = ∇log p) need their update *re-expressed* in RF terms — usually via `z0|t = z_t − t·v` (the predicted-clean latent) and the velocity↔score conversion. A method that *only* ships DDPM code is a recipe to re-derive, not a drop-in.
2. **Waveform-VAE latent, not mel.** SA3 works in the SAME autoencoder's 256-dim latent at ~10.77 Hz. The large older audio body (AudioLDM/Tango/MusicControlNet lineage) is mel-spectrogram + vocoder; those architectures do not transfer, though their *control representations* often do.

**A third rule cuts across everything:** every CFG-based or inversion-based method is **inert or near-useless on the APT-distilled few-step post-trained checkpoints** (`rf_denoiser`, cfg inert, 1–4 steps). They all want the multi-step, cfg-live `-base` checkpoints. Steering research happens on `-base`; only after it works do you ask whether it survives distillation.

**Legend.** ⏱ **train** / **infer** · maturity 🟢 ships-with-code · 🟡 research-code · 🟠 paper-only · 🔴 speculative · transfer **RF** ✅native ⚠️needs-rework ✘DDPM-only · **WAV** ✅latent-ok ⚠️different-latent ✘mel-only · recommendation from the adversarial pass: **core / useful / fringe**.

**A note on the differential-attention trap.** SA3's attention is *differential* (two softmax maps subtracted → **signed** maps, not probability distributions). Every method that reads, maximizes, or reweights raw attention maps (P2P-reweight, Attend-and-Excite, ReFlex's map adaptation) has its core operation break on signed maps and must be re-derived. The one attention method that sidesteps this entirely (HeadRouter, by routing *which heads* get guidance rather than editing maps) is flagged accordingly.

## Ch4 — Sigma, and the per-moment field (Ch15 frontier)

*Question the book asks: does the model tolerate non-uniform molten-ness across a clip? **The survey's answer is yes** — DiTs demonstrably accept per-token noise levels.*

- **Patch Forcing** — *Denoising, Fast and Slow* (CVPR 2026) · [2604.19141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19141) · [code](https://github.com/CompVis/patch-forcing) · ⏱train · 🟡 · RF⚠️ WAV✅ · **core**. Trains per-patch timesteps into adaLN + a difficulty head → a *learned* structured sigma field. On SA3 "per-patch" becomes "per-frame" directly. The only candidate that yields a learned field rather than a hand-set schedule. ⚠️ Verify the released checkpoints are actually flow-matching before assuming a clean RF port; needs a fine-tune (you have the machinery); confirm differential attention + 64 memory tokens tolerate per-token adaLN — untested anywhere. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** none of the newest sweep's 14 papers train per-patch/per-frame timesteps into adaLN or otherwise produce a learned structured sigma field; DEMON and Diffusion Warm Initialization (both below) are inference-time re-melt mechanisms, not training interventions on adaLN, so they neither confirm nor challenge this entry's claim of being the only "learned field" candidate. Assessment stands unchanged.
- **Time-to-Move (dual-clock denoising)** — [2511.08633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08633) · [code](https://github.com/time-to-move/TTM) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. *Verifier correction:* it's per-region **denoising start-index**, not literal SDEdit re-noise, and it **already supports a flow-matching backbone (Wan 2.2)** — so it transfers to SA3-`base` with almost no noise-math rewrite. Generalizes your binary masked-SDEdit to a graded per-frame start time on the `local_add_cond` mask. The cleanest training-free realization of "per-moment molten-ness." **Revisit 2026-07-06:** checked DEMON (below) for overlap since it also does per-frame-adjustable control at inference time. DEMON's mechanism is a per-frame *blend-target* (anchor-to-source vs anchor-to-prediction ratio) read fresh at each shared solver step — not a per-region denoising start-index / graded start-time on a shared mask the way this method does it (confirmed against DEMON's own text: "Per-frame source blending... a framewise transformation-strength axis"). Different mechanism, same general spirit, but not evidence for or against this entry's specific claim. No delta.
- **Stochastic Generation for Flow Models (RF→VP-SDE kernel)** — *Inference-Time Scaling for Flow Models* (NeurIPS 2025) · [2503.19385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19385) · [code](https://flow-inference-time-scaling.github.io/) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **core (enabler)**. Provides the formula + code to convert SA3's deterministic linear-interpolant ODE into an equivalent VP-SDE path — the *legitimate* way to re-inject noise (churn/restart) on a model trained as a straight ODE. **Discard the reward/Rollover-Budget-Forcing search layer** (not needed for free-form editing); keep the interpolant-conversion kernel. Restart and per-region re-melt only become RF-correct once this is in place. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** no new paper offers or challenges an RF-to-SDE-kernel conversion. Diffusion Warm Initialization (below) uses a DDPM/VE framing directly (confirmed verbatim in its PDF: "In this work, we use VE"), not RF, so it doesn't test or corroborate this kernel's specific conversion claim — it operates in a different (older) formalism throughout.
- **Restart Sampling** (NeurIPS 2023) · [2306.14878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14878) · [code](https://github.com/Newbeeer/diffusion_restart_sampling) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✅ · **useful**. The canonical restart algorithm + error-contraction framing, but EDM/VP-only and global-stage intervals; on RF it must go through the kernel above. Cite it for the idea, run it through #2503.19385. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** no new paper touches restart/churn algorithms specifically.
- **Iterative Partial Refinement** · [2605.19317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19317) · [code](https://github.com/ahn-ml/IPR) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **fringe**. *Verifier correction:* code **does** exist (the finder said paper-only), **but** it's demonstrated on discrete Sudoku-style reasoning, not continuous latents — the "regions = time spans on `local_add_cond`" framing is pure extrapolation. Overlaps Time-to-Move, which is already continuous-latent. Cite for the verifier-free selective-remelt concept only. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** no new paper bears on the discrete-reasoning-vs-continuous-latent gap already flagged here.
- **Region-Adaptive vector-σ diffusion** · [2604.01122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01122) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF⚠️ · the mathematical statement of a per-element sigma field (forward process with `diag(σ_t)`), but DDPM-framed, no code, built for an image codec. The design blueprint if you want a *trained* continuous sigma field; Patch Forcing is the actionable version. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** still the only per-element-sigma-field design blueprint on file; no new paper offers a trained continuous sigma field with code, so its role (design statement only, Patch Forcing is the actionable version) is unchanged.
- **DEMON** — *Diffusion Engine for Musical Orchestrated Noise* · [2605.28657](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28657) · [code](https://github.com/daydreamlive/DEMON) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **fringe (production reference, not a method to build)**. *Added 2026-07-06.* A live-performance streaming-systems paper, not a control-method paper: turns ACE-Step (DiT + flow matching, Oobleck-lineage waveform VAE — architecturally closer to SA3 than most of this survey, though the book's own Stable Audio Open entries elsewhere — stable-audio-controlnet, MGE-LDM, FreeSliders — are literal siblings of SA3 and closer still) into a 12.3-generations/sec playable instrument (RTX 5090) via per-slot heterogeneous denoise schedules and shared-mutable per-step state. *Read this carefully before citing it as confirmation of the chapter's per-moment-field claim — it isn't quite that:* the heterogeneity proven live is (a) across different in-flight generations in a ring buffer, each with its own fixed schedule baked at admission, and (b) a per-frame **blend-target** curve (anchor-to-source vs anchor-to-prediction, read fresh every solver step) — not a literal per-frame **noise level**. What it does establish: per-frame-adjustable control curves are fast enough to be musically interactive on a flow-matching DiT at this scale, which is real (if oblique) supporting evidence for the section's opening claim. File as the production-systems companion to Patch Forcing/Time-to-Move — useful for "is this buildable at speed," not for "has per-frame sigma been proven live."
- **Diffusion Warm Initialization** · [2606.18968](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18968) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✘ WAV⚠️ · **fringe**. *Added 2026-07-06.* A systematic empirical study of exactly the "where you start is a choice" question this chapter poses: sweeps SDEdit's `tau_init` on Stable Audio Open using a pitch-Jaccard-distance (melodic faithfulness) + FAD (realism) grid to locate a sweet spot (~0.8, JD<0.6/FAD<0.7), and finds re-adding noise to the guide (λ=1) is usually unnecessary and can hurt vs. treating the guide as already on the intermediate marginal (λ=0). The mechanism itself is Longform's already-shipped whole-segment SDEdit re-anchor — not a per-moment field, and no new capability — and it's DDPM/VE on Stable Audio Open's own VAE, not RF on SAME, so nothing ports directly. *Verifier note:* fails both hard filters as-is; no public code, only a companion audio-examples site. The one transferable idea is methodological: a metric-sweep harness (a LatCH chroma/onset faithfulness score + a FAD-analog, swept against SA3's own rectified-flow `t` axis) to pick a re-anchor timestep empirically instead of by ear — worth a line if re-anchor timestep selection is ever revisited, not an action item now.

## Ch5 — Attention as form

*The book asks what else could walk through the cross-attention door. Verdict: attention control transfers to SA3 only through the 2024–26 RF/DiT-native re-derivations — never the DDPM-era originals — and the differential-attention trap governs all of them.*

- **ReFlex** — *Text-Guided Editing in Rectified Flow* (ICCV 2025) · [2507.01496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01496) · [code](https://github.com/wlaud1001/ReFlex) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. The only attention-injection method built *natively* for rectified-flow transformers (mid-step partial inversion for the linear trajectory; extracts self-attn / cross-attn / residual features and adapts rather than hard-copies). Built for MM-DiT, so remap the I2I-SA / I2T-CA split onto SA3's separate self-attn(latents)+cross-attn(T5Gemma) — which is actually *cleaner* on SA3. ⚠️ Map-adaptation step needs re-derivation for signed differential-attention maps; needs a usable RF inversion (`-base` only).
- **HeadRouter** (CVPR 2025) · [2411.15034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15034) · [code](https://github.com/ICTMCG/HeadRouter) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. Routes text guidance to the *heads* most responsible for an edit instead of editing maps — **the one method that dodges the differential-attention trap entirely**. Genuinely new vs LatCH/TADA (those steer latents/activations, not attention-head allocation). The probe writes itself: correlate each head's attention with your per-frame MIR DB to label which heads carry timbre/onset/chroma, *then* route. **Revisit 2026-07-06 addendum:** TADA (2602.11910, now deep-read/verified real, see the new entry below) gives a causal, numeric answer to "which layers carry concept control" on the SA3-closest sibling (Stable Audio Open, 24 cross-attn layers matching SA3's own depth): layers {12,13} alone are the bottleneck, ablating the rest costs almost nothing. Complementary to HeadRouter (TADA localizes by layer + steers by activation addition, not by routing attention), but it de-risks the "probe writes itself" plan — start the head-correlation probe around blocks 12-13 rather than scanning all 24 blind. Caveat: TADA's localization is cross-attention only, no timestep dimension, measured on the sibling model not SA3, and is about where to steer activations, not head-routing per se — suggestive, not proof the same bottleneck governs HeadRouter's mechanism. **Amendment 2026-07-06 (CONTINUITY's precision fix):** TADA's {12,13} was localized on Stable Audio Open, whose conv-VAE latent is *acoustics-only*; SA3 runs on SAME latents with semantics **trained in** (flow-matching alignment loss + linear chroma/ILD regressors + contrastive text alignment — `papers/arxiv-2605.18613.md`). SA3's DiT may therefore need to build *less* internal semantic structure than Stable Audio Open's, so its own bottleneck could sit elsewhere or be more diffuse — {12,13} is a **prior for search ordering** (probe mid-stack first — independently corroborated by Stability's own discriminator tapping layer 14 of medium's 24 for its feature head, `papers/arxiv-2605.17991.md`), **not a verified substitute for the full 24-layer scan**. Probe mid-stack first, but run the full scan regardless — do not skip it on this evidence alone. Cross-cutting caution for Ch11: the same layers are bad places to confine a trained weight-space adapter (Concept Sliders -21% AUC when localized there) — "where to steer" and "where to confine trainable capacity" are different questions.
- **Attend-and-Excite** (SIGGRAPH 2023) · [2301.13826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13826) · [code](https://github.com/yuval-alaluf/Attend-and-Excite) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅(idea) WAV✅ · **useful**. Gradient step on `z_t` to amplify the most-neglected prompt token → compositional faithfulness ("ensure *both* the cello *and* the rain are present") — a capability LatCH/TADA don't give, and it rides your existing TFG infrastructure. ⚠️ Max-activation objective breaks on signed maps; retune scale for the RF trajectory.
- **EditGen** · [2507.11096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11096) · [code](https://github.com/billsioros/EditGen) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **useful**. Confirms the P2P **replace/refine/reweight** recipe works on text→audio cross-attention (SA3's exact topology). *Caveat:* validated on **MusicGen (autoregressive)**, and its only diffusion baseline (mel Auffusion) *under*performed — so borrow the token-reweight idea, ignore the AR plumbing (an iterative RF model uses vanilla per-step injection).
- **Dual-Channel Attention Guidance** · [2602.18022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18022) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · **fringe**. *Verifier flag:* the finder mis-framed it as a self/cross split; the real mechanism is a Key-vs-Value channel decomposition, orthogonal to self/cross — the optimistic "matches SA3 topology" claim does not hold. Single-author, no code.
- **P2P / Plug-and-Play / MasaCtrl** · [2208.01626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01626) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ · **background**. The design vocabulary (replace/refine/reweight; structure injection; mutual self-attn) but DDPM/DDIM + UNet — you get this capability through ReFlex/FlowEdit instead. Read, don't build.
- **TADA — Tuning Audio Diffusion Models through Activation Steering** · [2602.11910](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11910) · ⏱both · 🟠 · RF✅(model-agnostic) WAV✅ · **core**. *Added 2026-07-06.* This file already assumed the reader knew TADA (see the "LatCH/TADA" asides on HeadRouter and Attend-and-Excite above, and the Ch5/6 section header) but never gave it its own entry — now deep-read and verified real, here it is. Activation-patches cross-attention K/V with counterfactual prompt pairs (vocal gender, tempo, mood, instrument, genre swaps; MuQ/CLAP-scored) across Stable Audio Open (24 cross-attn layers — SA3's own DiT depth, the closest analog), Ace-Step, and AudioLDM2, and finds a **causal semantic bottleneck** in every architecture tested: on the SA3-closest sibling, layers **{12,13} of 24** alone carry essentially all measured concept control — ablating everything else costs almost nothing (causally verified, not correlational). Restricting CAA/AUSteer/SAE activation steering to those layers beats unrestricted steering by +46–49% AUC and beats prompt-space (PCI), score-space (FreeSliders), and weight-space (Concept Sliders) alternatives outright. ⚠️ Two-edged finding, cross-cutting into Ch11: the same localization that helps *activation* steering **actively hurts weight-space adapters** — Concept Sliders trained only on the bottleneck layers lost −21% AUC and −75% smoothness (their read: adapters add new mechanisms, they don't want to be confined to where existing mechanisms concentrate). Localization is cross-attention-only (self-attn/MLP unmapped) and patches all timesteps at once (no crispness/last-25%-of-denoising answer yet). Directly actionable for this section: before building HeadRouter's head-correlation probe from scratch, cross-check whether SA3 blocks ~12–13 alone already carry most of the concept-control signal — this is a much cheaper first experiment than scanning all 24 blocks blind. (See the fuller entry with steering-method benchmarks in Ch5/6 below.)

## Ch5/6 — Activation steering & representation engineering (TADA's neighbours, Plan B)

*Where TADA adds one fixed direction, this family offers an unsupervised, disentangled feature **dictionary** — and per-timestep directions, which a single static vector ignores.*

- **Steering Diffusion Transformers with SAEs** · [OpenReview J48XM0au4u](https://openreview.net/forum?id=J48XM0au4u) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **high (caveated)**. Trains SAEs on DiT residual-stream/block-update states, steers by injecting decoder atoms — tested on **Flux + SD3 (both RF DiTs)**, the closest architectural match. Gives a monosemantic feature dictionary you have no equivalent of. ⚠️ **Withdrawn from ICLR 2026**, no released code/arXiv id; the same authors' earlier [sdxl-unbox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22366) (U-Net era) is the usable starting code. You'd train SAEs on your own 202k-latent activation dumps. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** TADA (2602.11910, deep-read/verified real — see the new entry below) reproduces this entry's core mechanism — SAE trained on DiT activations, steering via decoder-atom injection — and gets it working end-to-end on a real, flow-matching (RF-native per the paper's own text) audio DiT: Ace-Step. There, localized TopK-SAE steering reaches best-overall-AUC (0.106 MuQ/0.059 CLAP) among every method tested, beating weight/score/prompt-space alternatives. IMPORTANT SCOPE CORRECTION: TADA's SAE-steering method itself was only trained and benchmarked on Ace-Step (Table 1/13) — Stable Audio Open's role in TADA is limited to confirming, via activation patching, that the same 2-layer cross-attn bottleneck exists there too (Sec. 4); the SAE/decoder-atom steering was not run on SAO. Also worth flagging, not resolving here: TADA's own text states AudioLDM2 and Stable Audio Open are trained with plain diffusion loss in its setup while Ace-Step is flow-matching — this cuts against this sourcebook's other entries (FreeSliders, stable-audio-controlnet) that call Stable Audio Open an "RF DiT"; worth a cross-check. Treat TADA (via Ace-Step) as the working audio-native, RF-native reference for the SAE-steering recipe; use the withdrawn OpenReview paper only for its residual-stream/block-update (vs TADA's cross-attn-only) formulation.
- **TIDE — Temporal-Aware SAEs for DiTs** · [2503.07050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07050) · ⏱both · 🟠 · RF⚠️ WAV⚠️ · **high**. Timestep-conditioned SAE (scale(t)/shift(t) MLP) → **per-timestep steerable directions** — the direct answer to "find time-varying directions," and a natural dovetail with LatCH-B. Image-only, no code; refit the timestep MLP to RF's continuous t∈[0,1]. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** verified directly in the TADA paper ("we run all the methods within all denoising steps"; the only timestep-related ablation is *when to start* a fixed direction, not a time-varying direction — Table 15). TADA (2602.11910), the strongest audio-native activation-steering result to date, confirms it does not test a timestep dimension; this sharpens rather than closes TIDE's rationale — the time-varying-direction gap TIDE targets is still unaddressed by the newest evidence.
- **FreeSliders** · [2511.00103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00103) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **high**. Training-free concept sliders from a contrastive prompt pair (the RF-equivalent of a CFG-style offset added to `v_θ`), with auto-saturation detection. **Its audio experiments run on Stable Audio Open — your sibling model** — the strongest "this already runs on your kind of model" evidence in the whole survey. Costs 2–3 extra forward passes/step; `-base` only (contrast collapses on distilled). Confirm a real repo before depending on it (project page only). **Revisit 2026-07-06:** TADA (2602.11910) does directly benchmark against FreeSliders, but the head-to-head (Table 1/2, human eval) is run on Ace-Step, NOT Stable Audio Open — TADA's "Benchmark Details" section explicitly adapts all baselines to Ace-Step for the main comparison; per-concept tables (20-28) are all labeled "with Ace-Step," and the human-eval breakdown uses Ace-Step's {7,8} layer tags. On Ace-Step, localized activation steering (CAA/SAE) beats FreeSliders on AUC (0.106/0.059 vs FreeSliders' 0.073/0.047 MuQ/CLAP) and on human "Seamless Edit" ratings. FreeSliders remains real, training-free, and — per its own paper (Ezra et al., cited by TADA as ref [17]) — audio-validated specifically on Stable Audio Open; that claim is untouched by TADA. So: FreeSliders is no longer the strongest audio-native evidence in this section overall (TADA/Ace-Step is), but it has not been beaten on its own SAO turf — correct the entry's framing from "benchmarked head-to-head on Stable Audio Open" to "benchmarked head-to-head on Ace-Step, a sibling audio DiT."

## Ch8 — The conditioner: a native time-varying channel

*The book's claim that a measured **number** can be first-class conditioning is exactly right, and the field has converged on **two** injection styles for time-aligned control: a single linear-add at the input latent, or a copied-and-reduced ControlNet branch with additive per-layer injection. **Nobody uses adaLN for time-varying control** — adaLN is reserved for global/scalar conditioning, matching SA3's use of it only for `seconds_total`. This validates `local_add_cond` as your injection point.* **Revisit 2026-07-06:** SegTune (2606.02638, below) shows a third time-aligned conditioning pattern in the wild — per-segment NL-caption broadcast, channel-concatenated with a global embedding — for song structure/emotion control, not numeric MIR features. The "two injection styles" claim holds for numeric per-frame control (chroma/loudness/pitch, this section's actual focus) but should read as "the two dominant patterns for numeric control" rather than an exhaustive claim about all time-varying conditioning.

- **Sketch2Sound** (ICASSP 2025) · [2412.08550](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08550) · ⏱both · 🟢(Adobe Firefly) · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. A **single trainable linear projection** of each control (loudness, centroid, pitch) **added to the noisy latent at the VAE frame rate** — provably *identical* to your `local_add_cond` pattern, on a Stable-Audio-lineage DiT. Its pitch control is a direct precedent for your chroma signal. *Verifier corrections:* it never literally names rectified flow (RF inferred from lineage), and it **fully fine-tunes** the DiT — on the APT-distilled checkpoint that risks the distillation, so adapt to **frozen-base + projection-only / LoRA** against `-base`. No public code; ~50-line reimplement. **Steal the median-filtering-of-controls-during-training trick** — free robustness to loosely-specified inputs. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** no new evidence bears on this entry; assessment stands.
- **JASCO** (ISMIR 2024) · [2406.10970](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10970) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/blob/main/docs/JASCO.md) · ⏱train · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **core**. Conditional **flow matching** (`L=(1+t)·L_CFM`) with chords/drums/melody **concatenated along the feature dim** at the input, plus an **information-bottleneck + temporal-blur** that prevents one control leaking into other attributes — directly portable to disentangle your MIR features. *Caveat:* EnCodec latent at 50 Hz (not the SAME 10.77 Hz), 330M U-Net-ish backbone — the **recipe** transfers, not weights; resample controls to 10.77 Hz. **Revisit 2026-07-06:** verified against SegTune's (2606.02638) actual ablation (Table 2, PDF): concatenation of global+segment Qwen3 embeddings beats a linearly-mixed-weight fusion (weights 0.2/0.8) on nearly every reported metric (G-MuLan 0.47 vs 0.43, S-MuLan 0.38 vs 0.35, gender-control 96.7% vs 90.5%, and every SongEval sub-score) — the paper's own text calls the mixed setting's drop "clear." This independently corroborates JASCO's choice to concatenate control features along the feature dim rather than mix/sum them, on an adjacent task (song generation) with different conditioning content. No architectural change to the JASCO recommendation itself, just added external support for the fusion mechanism.
- **Music ControlNet** (TASLP 2024) · [2311.07069](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07069) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **useful**. The canonical melody=chroma / dynamics=energy / rhythm=onset triplet **and the partial-in-time masking strategy** (specify a control over only part of the timeline, model improvises the rest) — the best teaching template for Ch8/Ch12. Mel-UNet + DDPM + vocoder, so **design only, nothing architectural ports**.
- **DiT melody ControlNet with top-k CQT** (ICASSP 2025) · [2410.05151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05151) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF✘(v-objective) WAV✅ · **useful**. The load-bearing idea: **top-k CQT beats single-vector chroma** for polyphonic/multi-track melody (less octave/voicing ambiguity) — worth knowing as you commit chroma to `local_add_cond`. - **MusiConGen** · [2407.15060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15060) · [code](https://github.com/Cyan0731/MusiConGen) · ⏱train · 🟢 · **low**. MusicGen (AR) finetune; only its chord/rhythm-extraction + BPM-conditioning UX is a reference, not the method.
- **SegTune** (Kling Team, Kuaishou/USTC/PKU) · [2606.02638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02638) · ⏱train · 🟢 [code](https://github.com/KlingAIResearch/SegTune) (train/infer/eval scripts released 2026-05-29; accepted ACL 2026 Oral, Best-Paper nominee) · RF✅ WAV✅(lineage) · **useful**. *Added 2026-07-06.* A song-generation DiT (1.1B params, 16 LLaMA-style blocks) on genuine conditional flow matching (`x_t=(1-t)x0+t*x1`, target velocity `u=x1-x0`) + the Stable-Audio-lineage 1D VAE (21.5 Hz; Evans et al. 2024a, ISMIR "Long-form music generation with latent diffusion" — same core author line as SAME, its direct predecessor) whose conditioning pathway is a third pattern beside Sketch2Sound's linear-add and JASCO's concat-at-input: hierarchical **global** (whole-song genre/timbre/emotion) + **per-segment** (verse/chorus instrumentation/rhythm/emotional-transition) natural-language captions, encoded by the same Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B LLM text embedder, then **channel-concatenated** (not summed or linearly mixed) and broadcast only within each segment's own time window (window boundaries from a separately fine-tuned Qwen3-4B-Base duration-predictor LLM). The transferable kernel is narrow but real: per-segment NL-caption broadcast + concat-fusion as a structure/emotion-conditioning idea to sit beside chroma/MIR `local_add_cond` work — a different content-type (text, not numeric features) than Sketch2Sound/JASCO/Music-ControlNet's melody-chroma-dynamics lane.
- **UNISON — depth-matched text injection** · [2605.31530](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31530) · ⏱train · 🟢 [code](https://github.com/lizhaoqing/UNISON) + [checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/jac22/UNISON) · RF✅ WAV✘(MMAudio continuous VAE: waveform→mel-spectrogram(STFT)→latent, fails hard filter 2) · **fringe (idea only)**. *Added 2026-07-06.* Ablation on this flow-matching MM-DiT (Table 8, verified): projecting *different layers* of a frozen Qwen2.5-Omni-7B instruction-LLM encoder into depth-matched DiT blocks (shallow DiT blocks ← shallow/lexical encoder layers, deep blocks ← deep/semantic layers) beats broadcasting a single **penultimate**-layer embedding to every block (FD 20.46 vs 22.71, CLAP 0.180 vs 0.175 at matched compute, D24 config). (2) Code and a trained checkpoint are public (not "🟡 research-code" undersold, and not the "🟠 no-code" status implied elsewhere) — upgraded to 🟢. Worth a cheap `-base` ablation — feed 2–3 T5Gemma layers to shallow/mid/deep blocks instead of one — not a roadmap commitment.

## Ch13 — Control heads & training-free guidance (your native territory)

*The decisive framing for the book: **your LatCH-F pipeline is a special case of TFG's design space**, and TFG subsumes DPS/FreeDoM/Universal-Guidance/MPGD. Present TFG as the unifying theory, LatCH as one instantiation, then list the knobs you're leaving on the table.*

DEMON is a live-system existence-proof that per-frame control signals read fresh at every solver step can be delivered with 1-tick onset at real-time speed — evidence the *plumbing* for an always-on loop is buildable — but it explicitly makes no musicality, stability, or listening-test claims, so it doesn't touch the oscillation/gain-scheduling question itself. Neither paper settles which side of the engineering/fundamental fault line the continuous-feedback frontier sits on; both sharpen the question rather than answer it.

## Ch16 — Dreaming up new controls

### Coupled / constrained: "raise X, pin Y" (the coupled-head dream)

- **HardFlow** · [2511.08425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08425) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **high**. Hard-constrained sampling on flow matching via MPC: satisfy a differentiable constraint **exactly at terminal time** without forcing the whole path onto the manifold. - **Projected Coupled Diffusion (PCD)** (ICLR 2026) · [2508.10531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10531) · [code](https://github.com/) (linked from paper) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF⚠️ WAV✅ · **high**. - **SuperDiff** (ICLR 2025) · [2412.17762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17762) · [code](https://github.com/necludov/super-diffusion) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **high**. ## Honesty ledger — corrections the adversarial pass made

These are where a finder's optimism was wrong and the verifier caught it. Trust these over any cheerful framing elsewhere:

- **APG** shipped via the diffusers **Guider system (PR #10173)**, not the closed PR #9626; and it **must be applied on `z0|t`, not raw velocity**.
- **D-Flow** has **no official public repo** (both cited URLs are dead/wrong) — reimplement from the paper.
- **Restart Sampling** is **EDM/VP-only**, not RF — route it through the RF→VP-SDE kernel ([2503.19385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19385)).
- **Iterative Partial Refinement** code **does** exist, but it's demonstrated on **Sudoku-style discrete reasoning**, not continuous audio latents — the audio framing was extrapolation.
- **Time-to-Move** **already supports a flow-matching backbone** (the finder wrongly said DDPM-only) — it transfers to SA3 *more* easily than first stated.
- **Self Forcing / Rolling Forcing** are built on **Wan (an RF model)**, so they're already proven on an RF base — transfer is *less* risky than first stated.
- **FreeAudio**'s base is a **Flan-T5 16 kHz DiT, not Stable Audio Open** (SAO is only a comparison baseline); no code.
- **AudEdit** is a **single-author preprint with no code/demo/samples/review** — the most on-target hit, but you reimplement it.
- **Self-Transcendence**'s real title differs, and it's a **from-scratch-training** recipe, awkward for fine-tuning an existing checkpoint.
- **Dual-Channel Attention Guidance** is a **Key/Value** decomposition, *not* the self/cross split the finder claimed — its "matches SA3 topology" justification is void.
- **Sketch2Sound / top-k CQT / Music ControlNet** never literally confirm rectified flow (lineage-inferred or v-objective/DDPM); only Sketch2Sound's *conditioning topology* is the safe transfer.
- *(2026-07-06 revisit)* **TADA** (2602.11910) was initially mis-flagged as fabricated because an unrelated real paper shares the identical acronym — now independently confirmed real via arXiv 2602.11910v2 and the archived PDF; its Stable-Audio-Open results are real but narrower than a first read implies (localization-only, not the full steering-method benchmark — see Ch5/Ch5-6 entries).
- *(2026-07-06 revisit)* **SegTune** and **UNISON** were both initially undersold: SegTune's training/eval code is public (not "no code"), and UNISON both mislabeled RF as ✘ (it's a genuine linear-interpolant rectified flow) and undersold its maturity (code + checkpoint are public, not research-code-only).
- *(2026-07-06 revisit)* **Cautious Weight Decay** is not a "formalization" of the modded-nanogpt community sign-masking trick — it's a distinct extension of a different, earlier academic paper's idea (arXiv:2411.16085) applied to weight decay instead of the update; separately, masking the orthogonalized Muon update itself already exists as informal community prior art (two GitHub PRs), contrary to an initial "unclaimed territory" framing.
- *(2026-07-06 revisit)* **Diffusion Forcing / DFoT**'s "streaming" leg was singled out as the one part of its three-leg rationale (streaming, LatCH-B, sigma-field) with a materially cheaper competing path (Live Music Diffusion Models' <8-GPU-hour finetune) — the other two legs are unaffected; DEMON was considered and rejected as a second competing path since the paper explicitly disclaims indefinite-continuation capability and contains no time-axis attention KV-caching.

**Cross-cutting caveats that apply to nearly everything:** (1) image-tuned hyperparameters (step windows, momentum/rescale, LQR weights, guidance scales) all need an audio sweep on the 256-dim/10.77 Hz latent; (2) the differential-attention signed-map trap breaks every raw-attention-map method; (3) every CFG/inversion method is `-base`-only — dead on the APT-distilled few-step checkpoints.
