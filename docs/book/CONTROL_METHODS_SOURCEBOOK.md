# The Control-Methods Sourcebook

*Companion to **[Hands Inside the Instrument](README.md)**. The book builds intuition and ends each chapter with an open question; this file is what you reach for once you know **which** control you want and need the real artifacts — papers, code, experiments — to build it. Every entry is judged against SA3's exact construction, not diffusion-in-general.*

*Compiled June 2026 from a 14-lane literature sweep, each lane adversarially verified against the two hard filters below. Where a verifier corrected an over-optimistic claim, the correction is kept here rather than the cheerful version.*

---

## How to read this

**The two hard filters** decide whether a method transfers at all. Most of the diffusion-control literature fails one of them:

1. **Rectified flow, not DDPM.** SA3's `-base` checkpoints carve along a straight-line velocity field `z_t = (1-t)·z0 + t·noise`. Methods derived for DDPM/score-SDE ("add noise to σ", ancestral sampling, score = ∇log p) need their update *re-expressed* in RF terms — usually via `z0|t = z_t − t·v` (the predicted-clean latent) and the velocity↔score conversion. A method that *only* ships DDPM code is a recipe to re-derive, not a drop-in.
2. **Waveform-VAE latent, not mel.** SA3 works in the SAME autoencoder's 256-dim latent at ~10.77 Hz. The large older audio body (AudioLDM/Tango/MusicControlNet lineage) is mel-spectrogram + vocoder; those architectures do not transfer, though their *control representations* often do.

**A third rule cuts across everything:** every CFG-based or inversion-based method is **inert or near-useless on the APT-distilled few-step post-trained checkpoints** (`rf_denoiser`, cfg inert, 1–4 steps). They all want the multi-step, cfg-live `-base` checkpoints. Steering research happens on `-base`; only after it works do you ask whether it survives distillation.

**Legend.** ⏱ **train** / **infer** · maturity 🟢 ships-with-code · 🟡 research-code · 🟠 paper-only · 🔴 speculative · transfer **RF** ✅native ⚠️needs-rework ✘DDPM-only · **WAV** ✅latent-ok ⚠️different-latent ✘mel-only · recommendation from the adversarial pass: **core / useful / fringe**.

**A note on the differential-attention trap.** SA3's attention is *differential* (two softmax maps subtracted → **signed** maps, not probability distributions). Every method that reads, maximizes, or reweights raw attention maps (P2P-reweight, Attend-and-Excite, ReFlex's map adaptation) has its core operation break on signed maps and must be re-derived. The one attention method that sidesteps this entirely (HeadRouter, by routing *which heads* get guidance rather than editing maps) is flagged accordingly.

## Ch4 — Sigma, and the per-moment field (Ch15 frontier)

*Question the book asks: does the model tolerate non-uniform molten-ness across a clip? **The survey's answer is yes** — DiTs demonstrably accept per-token noise levels.*

- **Patch Forcing** — *Denoising, Fast and Slow* (CVPR 2026) · [2604.19141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19141) · [code](https://github.com/CompVis/patch-forcing) · ⏱train · 🟡 · RF⚠️ WAV✅ · **core**. Trains per-patch timesteps into adaLN + a difficulty head → a *learned* structured sigma field. On SA3 "per-patch" becomes "per-frame" directly. The only candidate that yields a learned field rather than a hand-set schedule. ⚠️ Verify the released checkpoints are actually flow-matching before assuming a clean RF port; needs a fine-tune (you have the machinery); confirm differential attention + 64 memory tokens tolerate per-token adaLN — untested anywhere.
- **Time-to-Move (dual-clock denoising)** — [2511.08633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08633) · [code](https://github.com/time-to-move/TTM) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. *Verifier correction:* it's per-region **denoising start-index**, not literal SDEdit re-noise, and it **already supports a flow-matching backbone (Wan 2.2)** — so it transfers to SA3-`base` with almost no noise-math rewrite. Generalizes your binary masked-SDEdit to a graded per-frame start time on the `local_add_cond` mask. The cleanest training-free realization of "per-moment molten-ness."
- **Stochastic Generation for Flow Models (RF→VP-SDE kernel)** — *Inference-Time Scaling for Flow Models* (NeurIPS 2025) · [2503.19385](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19385) · [code](https://flow-inference-time-scaling.github.io/) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **core (enabler)**. Provides the formula + code to convert SA3's deterministic linear-interpolant ODE into an equivalent VP-SDE path — the *legitimate* way to re-inject noise (churn/restart) on a model trained as a straight ODE. **Discard the reward/Rollover-Budget-Forcing search layer** (not needed for free-form editing); keep the interpolant-conversion kernel. Restart and per-region re-melt only become RF-correct once this is in place.
- **Restart Sampling** (NeurIPS 2023) · [2306.14878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14878) · [code](https://github.com/Newbeeer/diffusion_restart_sampling) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✅ · **useful**. The canonical restart algorithm + error-contraction framing, but EDM/VP-only and global-stage intervals; on RF it must go through the kernel above. Cite it for the idea, run it through #2503.19385.
- **Iterative Partial Refinement** · [2605.19317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19317) · [code](https://github.com/ahn-ml/IPR) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **fringe**. *Verifier correction:* code **does** exist (the finder said paper-only), **but** it's demonstrated on discrete Sudoku-style reasoning, not continuous latents — the "regions = time spans on `local_add_cond`" framing is pure extrapolation. Overlaps Time-to-Move, which is already continuous-latent. Cite for the verifier-free selective-remelt concept only.
- **Region-Adaptive vector-σ diffusion** · [2604.01122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01122) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF⚠️ · the mathematical statement of a per-element sigma field (forward process with `diag(σ_t)`), but DDPM-framed, no code, built for an image codec. The design blueprint if you want a *trained* continuous sigma field; Patch Forcing is the actionable version.

## Ch5 — Attention as form

*The book asks what else could walk through the cross-attention door. Verdict: attention control transfers to SA3 only through the 2024–26 RF/DiT-native re-derivations — never the DDPM-era originals — and the differential-attention trap governs all of them.*

- **ReFlex** — *Text-Guided Editing in Rectified Flow* (ICCV 2025) · [2507.01496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01496) · [code](https://github.com/wlaud1001/ReFlex) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. The only attention-injection method built *natively* for rectified-flow transformers (mid-step partial inversion for the linear trajectory; extracts self-attn / cross-attn / residual features and adapts rather than hard-copies). Built for MM-DiT, so remap the I2I-SA / I2T-CA split onto SA3's separate self-attn(latents)+cross-attn(T5Gemma) — which is actually *cleaner* on SA3. ⚠️ Map-adaptation step needs re-derivation for signed differential-attention maps; needs a usable RF inversion (`-base` only).
- **HeadRouter** (CVPR 2025) · [2411.15034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15034) · [code](https://github.com/ICTMCG/HeadRouter) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. Routes text guidance to the *heads* most responsible for an edit instead of editing maps — **the one method that dodges the differential-attention trap entirely**. Genuinely new vs LatCH/TADA (those steer latents/activations, not attention-head allocation). The probe writes itself: correlate each head's attention with your per-frame MIR DB to label which heads carry timbre/onset/chroma, *then* route.
- **Attend-and-Excite** (SIGGRAPH 2023) · [2301.13826](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13826) · [code](https://github.com/yuval-alaluf/Attend-and-Excite) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅(idea) WAV✅ · **useful**. Gradient step on `z_t` to amplify the most-neglected prompt token → compositional faithfulness ("ensure *both* the cello *and* the rain are present") — a capability LatCH/TADA don't give, and it rides your existing TFG infrastructure. ⚠️ Max-activation objective breaks on signed maps; retune scale for the RF trajectory.
- **EditGen** · [2507.11096](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11096) · [code](https://github.com/billsioros/EditGen) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **useful**. Confirms the P2P **replace/refine/reweight** recipe works on text→audio cross-attention (SA3's exact topology). *Caveat:* validated on **MusicGen (autoregressive)**, and its only diffusion baseline (mel Auffusion) *under*performed — so borrow the token-reweight idea, ignore the AR plumbing (an iterative RF model uses vanilla per-step injection).
- **Dual-Channel Attention Guidance** · [2602.18022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18022) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · **fringe**. *Verifier flag:* the finder mis-framed it as a self/cross split; the real mechanism is a Key-vs-Value channel decomposition, orthogonal to self/cross — the optimistic "matches SA3 topology" claim does not hold. Single-author, no code.
- **P2P / Plug-and-Play / MasaCtrl** · [2208.01626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.01626) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ · **background**. The design vocabulary (replace/refine/reweight; structure injection; mutual self-attn) but DDPM/DDIM + UNet — you get this capability through ReFlex/FlowEdit instead. Read, don't build.

## Ch5/6 — Activation steering & representation engineering (TADA's neighbours, Plan B)

*Where TADA adds one fixed direction, this family offers an unsupervised, disentangled feature **dictionary** — and per-timestep directions, which a single static vector ignores.*

- **Steering Diffusion Transformers with SAEs** · [OpenReview J48XM0au4u](https://openreview.net/forum?id=J48XM0au4u) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **high (caveated)**. Trains SAEs on DiT residual-stream/block-update states, steers by injecting decoder atoms — tested on **Flux + SD3 (both RF DiTs)**, the closest architectural match. Gives a monosemantic feature dictionary you have no equivalent of. ⚠️ **Withdrawn from ICLR 2026**, no released code/arXiv id; the same authors' earlier [sdxl-unbox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22366) (U-Net era) is the usable starting code. You'd train SAEs on your own 202k-latent activation dumps.
- **TIDE — Temporal-Aware SAEs for DiTs** · [2503.07050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07050) · ⏱both · 🟠 · RF⚠️ WAV⚠️ · **high**. Timestep-conditioned SAE (scale(t)/shift(t) MLP) → **per-timestep steerable directions** — the direct answer to "find time-varying directions," and a natural dovetail with LatCH-B. Image-only, no code; refit the timestep MLP to RF's continuous t∈[0,1].
- **FreeSliders** · [2511.00103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00103) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **high**. Training-free concept sliders from a contrastive prompt pair (the RF-equivalent of a CFG-style offset added to `v_θ`), with auto-saturation detection. **Its audio experiments run on Stable Audio Open — your sibling model** — the strongest "this already runs on your kind of model" evidence in the whole survey. Costs 2–3 extra forward passes/step; `-base` only (contrast collapses on distilled). Confirm a real repo before depending on it (project page only).
- **Concept Steerers (k-SAE on text embeddings)** · [2501.19066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19066) · [code](https://github.com/kim-dahye/steerers) · ⏱infer · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **medium**. Shipping code; steers the T5Gemma text embedding so the diffusion internals never matter. Cheap upstream slider — but acts only on text, so it can't reach pitch/chroma/per-frame structure (exactly what your LatCH+MIR stack targets).
- **Activation Patching / DiffMean for music** · [2504.04479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04479) · [code](https://github.com/gladia-research-group/music-mechint) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **medium**. The most audio-musical steering study (continuous tempo/timbre) with clean code — **but on MusicGen (autoregressive), not diffusion**. The DiffMean + layer-scan + injection-scale *methodology* ports; the vectors and "inject across AR steps" mechanic don't.
- **SAEdit** · [2510.05081](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05081) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · **low**. Token-level SAE editing of the conditioning sequence; same text-only ceiling as Concept Steerers, weaker novelty. (Ancestry worth knowing: Asyrp h-space [2210.10960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10960); Concept Sliders [2311.12092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12092).)

## Ch8 — The conditioner: a native time-varying channel

*The book's claim that a measured **number** can be first-class conditioning is exactly right, and the field has converged on **two** injection styles for time-aligned control: a single linear-add at the input latent, or a copied-and-reduced ControlNet branch with additive per-layer injection. **Nobody uses adaLN for time-varying control** — adaLN is reserved for global/scalar conditioning, matching SA3's use of it only for `seconds_total`. This validates `local_add_cond` as your injection point.*

- **Sketch2Sound** (ICASSP 2025) · [2412.08550](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08550) · ⏱both · 🟢(Adobe Firefly) · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. A **single trainable linear projection** of each control (loudness, centroid, pitch) **added to the noisy latent at the VAE frame rate** — provably *identical* to your `local_add_cond` pattern, on a Stable-Audio-lineage DiT. Its pitch control is a direct precedent for your chroma signal. *Verifier corrections:* it never literally names rectified flow (RF inferred from lineage), and it **fully fine-tunes** the DiT — on the APT-distilled checkpoint that risks the distillation, so adapt to **frozen-base + projection-only / LoRA** against `-base`. No public code; ~50-line reimplement. **Steal the median-filtering-of-controls-during-training trick** — free robustness to loosely-specified inputs.
- **JASCO** (ISMIR 2024) · [2406.10970](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10970) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/blob/main/docs/JASCO.md) · ⏱train · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **core**. Conditional **flow matching** (`L=(1+t)·L_CFM`) with chords/drums/melody **concatenated along the feature dim** at the input, plus an **information-bottleneck + temporal-blur** that prevents one control leaking into other attributes — directly portable to disentangle your MIR features. *Caveat:* EnCodec latent at 50 Hz (not the SAME 10.77 Hz), 330M U-Net-ish backbone — the **recipe** transfers, not weights; resample controls to 10.77 Hz.
- **Music ControlNet** (TASLP 2024) · [2311.07069](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07069) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF✘ WAV✘ · **useful**. The canonical melody=chroma / dynamics=energy / rhythm=onset triplet **and the partial-in-time masking strategy** (specify a control over only part of the timeline, model improvises the rest) — the best teaching template for Ch8/Ch12. Mel-UNet + DDPM + vocoder, so **design only, nothing architectural ports**.
- **DiT melody ControlNet with top-k CQT** (ICASSP 2025) · [2410.05151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05151) · ⏱train · 🟠 · RF✘(v-objective) WAV✅ · **useful**. The load-bearing idea: **top-k CQT beats single-vector chroma** for polyphonic/multi-track melody (less octave/voicing ambiguity) — worth knowing as you commit chroma to `local_add_cond`. Plus a curriculum-masking schedule for stable conditioner training. No code; base uses a v-objective, not RF.
- **MusiConGen** · [2407.15060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15060) · [code](https://github.com/Cyan0731/MusiConGen) · ⏱train · 🟢 · **low**. MusicGen (AR) finetune; only its chord/rhythm-extraction + BPM-conditioning UX is a reference, not the method.

## Ch9 — The dials: guidance and re-melting

### Guidance schedules (every one is `-base`-only — cfg is inert on distilled)

- **CFG-Zero\*** · [2503.18886](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18886) · [code](https://github.com/WeichenFan/CFG-Zero-star) · ⏱infer · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. The one CFG fix *derived for flow matching* (validated SD3/Flux/Wan), and **the repo ships a Qwen2.5-Omni audio pipeline** — audio is already demonstrated upstream. Two parts: optimized-scale `s* = ⟨v_c,v_u⟩/‖v_u‖²` and zero-init (zero the first ~4% of steps). Ship this first; it's a small patch.
- **APG (Adaptive Projected Guidance)** (ICLR 2025) · [2410.02416](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02416) · code: **diffusers Guider PR [#10173](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/pull/10173)** · ⏱infer · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. Decompose the CFG delta ∥/⊥ to `v_cond`, down-weight the parallel (over-saturation) part, + momentum + norm-rescale → raise cfg without timbre/dynamics over-bake. *Verifier corrections:* the finder's PR #9626 is **closed**; it shipped via the modular Guider system (#10173). **Must be applied on the denoised `z0|t`, not raw velocity** — convert `z0|t = z_t − t·v` first or the projection is in the wrong space. Re-tune momentum/rescale for audio.
- **Limited-interval guidance** (NeurIPS 2024) · [2404.07724](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07724) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **useful**. Guidance only in a middle noise window, off at both ends — a pure schedule, two bounds, no params. Overlaps CFG-Zero\*'s zero-init at the high-noise end, so treat early-off + interval as **one** combined schedule, not a stack.
- **CFG via Manifold Projection (CFG-MP)** (ICML 2026) · [2601.21892](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21892) · [code](https://github.com/LeonSuZhengYi/CFG-MP) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV⚠️ · **useful**. RF-native on-manifold projection of the guided update; heavier (inner loop per step). Most valuable as the **on-manifold stabilizer under stacked/coupled guidance** (Ch13/16), not as a first-line cfg fix.
- **Autoguidance** (NeurIPS 2024 oral) · [2406.02507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02507) · [code](https://github.com/NVlabs/edm2) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **useful**. Guide with a *worse* version of the same model → quality without diversity collapse. Uniquely feasible for you (you hold both SA3-small and -medium) — **but** the recipe wants a *degraded sibling of the same training run*; small vs medium are different depths/data, so the cleaner route is an **early/undertrained checkpoint of medium itself**. Validate the assumption before trusting it.
- **CADS** (ICLR 2024) · [2310.17347](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17347) · [code](https://github.com/v0xie/sd-webui-cads) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **medium**. Anneals noise on the *conditioning embedding* to restore diversity lost at high cfg — complements APG. Restrict the anneal to the **text tokens**, never `seconds_total` (which drives adaLN).

### Re-melting: inversion & inversion-free editing (also Ch10; unblocks the ZeroSep port)

- **FlowEdit** (ICCV 2025, Best Student Paper) · [2412.08629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08629) · [code](https://github.com/fallenshock/FlowEdit) · ⏱infer · 🟢 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. Inversion-free, optimization-free: integrate a source→target velocity-difference ODE. Validated on SD3/FLUX (SA3's RF family). The de-risked, code-backed template. Port: swap the 2D latent for SA3's 1D latent + the T5Gemma/seconds conditioning call; re-tune `n_max/n_min`; `-base` only.
- **AudEdit** · [2606.15149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15149) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core (de-risk first)**. **Literally FlowEdit ported onto Stable Audio 3 RF latents** — the single most on-target hit in the survey, with real metrics vs SDEdit/inversion/FireFlow (CLAP 0.42→0.52, FAD 65.7→50.4). *Verifier honesty:* single-author preprint (2026-06-13), **no code, no demo, no samples, no peer review** — you reimplement from a thin paper (math = a short FlowEdit variant, so low effort). FlowEdit (#3 above) is its working reference. **→ Status superseded (2026-06-22): `avp_sa3/scripts/sa3_flowsep.py` is already a near-faithful Algorithm 1 (3 gaps: `η` not exposed, no `n_min` tail, the `anchor_eta` name-collision). Full read + ranked plan + the entanglement caveat in [findings/2026-06-22-audedit-into-our-control-stack](findings/2026-06-22-audedit-into-our-control-stack.md).**
- **RF-Solver-Edit** (ICML 2025) · [2411.04746](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04746) · [code](https://github.com/wangjiangshan0725/RF-Solver-Edit) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **core**. The genuine **invert-to-noise-and-re-denoise** primitive you lack (vs FlowEdit, which never inverts) — this is "re-melting" in the book's sense, and the inversion stage a ZeroSep-style separator needs on RF. **Port the RF-Solver inversion half first** (pure ODE numerics, architecture-agnostic); the RF-Edit half shares vanilla self-attn K/V across passes → hits the differential-attention trap.
- **FireFlow** (ICML 2025) · [2412.07517](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07517) · [code](https://github.com/HolmesShuan/FireFlow-Fast-Inversion-of-Rectified-Flow-for-Image-Semantic-Editing) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **useful**. ~3× faster inversion (velocity-reuse, 2nd-order at 1-NFE cost) — a cheaper drop-in for the RF-Solver inversion half. The inversion *engine*, not a new capability.
- **RF-Inversion** (ICLR 2025) · [2410.10792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10792) · [code](https://github.com/LituRout/RF-Inversion) · ⏱infer · 🟡 · RF✅ WAV✅ · **useful**. LQR-controller inversion with an explicit **faithfulness↔editability dial** (`gamma`, `eta`, start/stop) — the RF-native answer to "SDEdit limits." Best as the inversion stage feeding your LatCH/TFG readout pipeline (blend its reference-pull with TFG on `z0|t`).
- **FlowAlign** · [2505.23145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23145) · ⏱infer · 🟠 · RF✅ · a terminal-point trajectory regularizer on FlowEdit that improves source consistency (for audio: keeps rhythm/transients/timbre). No code; a refinement layer once the FlowEdit/AudEdit port works — and your per-frame MIR/chroma DB is a natural consistency metric.

## Ch10 — Holding material still

Inpainting/clamping already ships (it's the `local_add_cond` mask).
